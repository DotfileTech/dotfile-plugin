Per member for the period I name: cases decided, checks reviewed, median and 90th-percentile time from case open to decision, and the trend against the previous period of equal length. Then the three whose queue grows faster than they clear it, and the three whose median time moved most. Always give the two numbers behind a trend, and say when a difference is too small to mean anything.
