Per template for the period I name: cases created, checks created, checks later deleted by a human, share auto-approved, share decided without a manually added check, median time to decision. Flag the templates whose checks get deleted most, and those where analysts routinely add a check the template did not create. For each, say which rule the pattern points to. Do not propose a change you cannot support with counts.
