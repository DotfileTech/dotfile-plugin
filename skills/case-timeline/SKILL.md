No case in context: ask which case, or find it with query_entities, before anything else. Explain this case's history. 1. Rebuild the chronology: entities created, template applied, checks created, results, reviews and decisions, communications, status and risk changes, controls. 2. For every step name the actor and its nature — a person, the API, the automation engine acting on a template rule, the portal contact, the system. Never state that something happened without saying what caused it. 3. Then answer the negative questions, which are the ones people actually ask: why a check was never created (which rule matched or did not, on which attribute), why the case was not auto-approved (which condition failed), why a step came late. 4. Where the record does not explain something, say so rather than constructing a plausible reason. An honest gap is useful; an invented cause is not. 5. Close with a short answer to my actual question, above the timeline.
