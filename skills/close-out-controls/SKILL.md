For each incomplete manual control, look for evidence in the case that satisfies it: an approved check, a note, a document, a completed communication. 3. Mark completed only where you can cite the specific evidence, and put that citation in the completion comment. 4. For the rest, say exactly what is missing and who must provide it. 5. Never complete a control you cannot evidence; never touch automatic controls.
